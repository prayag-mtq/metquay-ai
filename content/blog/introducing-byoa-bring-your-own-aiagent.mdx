## What is BYOA?

**BYOA (Bring Your Own Agent)** is a radical new paradigm that transforms how calibration operations integrate with artificial intelligence. Instead of forcing you to use a single, proprietary AI solution, metquay becomes the universal platform where *any* AI agent can plug in, collaborate, and automate your calibration workflows.

Think of it as the "operating system" for intelligent calibration agent-agnostic, infinitely scalable, and designed from the ground up for the autonomous future.

Whether you prefer Claude for its analytical prowess, GPT-4 for its versatility, Gemini for its data processing, or even your own custom-trained models metquay welcomes them all. No vendor lock-in. No compromises. Just pure, unbridled automation power.

## Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What if my favorite AI model isn't in the marketplace yet?** A: metquay's API is open and documented. You can connect any AI model that can make API calls. We also offer custom integration services.

  

**Q: Will my data be sent to third-party AI providers?** A: You control data flow. For sensitive data, we offer on-premises agent deployment where all AI processing happens within your infrastructure.

  

**Q: What happens if an agent makes a mistake?** A: All agent actions are logged and reversible. You can set approval thresholds for critical operations, and our QA agents provide multiple layers of validation.

  

**Q: Can I switch agents without disrupting operations?** A: Absolutely. Agents are hot-swappable. Deploy a new agent, test it in parallel, then switch traffic over when ready.

  

**Q: Do I need technical expertise to use BYOA?** A: No coding required. Our visual workflow builder is designed for calibration professionals, not programmers. Most users are operational within days.
