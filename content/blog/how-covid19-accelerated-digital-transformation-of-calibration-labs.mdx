### Unprecedented business conditions triggered the unprecedented adoption of digital transformation in calibration lab management.

<Figure src="/blog/how-covid19-accelerated-digital-transformation-of-calibration-labs/1.webp" alt="digital transformation of calibration labs " />

> Covid19 pandemic made the world realize the need and possibilities for more digitization. The calibration industry was no different. The year 2020 witnessed accelerated adoption of technology by the industry for process improvement and automation. This helped them survive the uncertain business conditions during the pandemic.

## 2020 started with uncertainties of unprecedented scale.

Labs struggled in the first quarter of 2020 when the pandemic struck. As with all other sectors, calibration businesses also were clueless on how to react to the emerging crisis. Employees were confined to their homes. Factories and Facilities were locked down and the equipment which used to run for a minimum of 8 hours a day was shut down for days and months.

People wondered if the sky was about to fall. Thanks to technology it didn’t.

## Cloud tech helped the labs to adapt

They realized that their current processes need to be changed to support people working from home. The only option before them was to adopt cloud-based applications for the digital transformation of calibration labs. And they adopted.

Automation helped much. For example, automated certification processes helped labs to issue certificates without much manual intervention. Timeline reminders to customers about due dates helped both parties manage time and resources efficiently. A section of their employees could work from home using such applications. Soon they adapted to the new normal.
