Our Story

We build the system of record for calibration labs

Metquay makes lab-management software for the calibration and metrology industry — the platform behind tracefii. We replace spreadsheets, paper worksheets and disconnected tools with one connected, audit-ready system: assets, works, worksheets, certificates and traceability, end to end.

Our approach is asset-centric and human-verified, supercharged by AI where it earns its place — adaptive calibration intervals, smarter scheduling, faster certificates — always with a person in the loop and an immutable trail behind every decision.

One belief drives all of it: a world where every measurement is trusted.

# How we work

# Our design methodology

We build the way the best product companies do,

design-led and customer-obsessed. Four principles guide every release.

Deep customer empathy

We design from the lab floor out — shadowing technicians, auditors and quality managers — before a line of code is written.

Go broad, then narrow

We explore many ways to solve a problem, then converge hard on the one that’s simplest and clearest for the lab.

Rapid experiments

We ship small, put it in front of real labs early, and let evidence — not opinion — decide what stays.

Trust by design

Every decision is judged against one bar: would it withstand an ISO 17025 audit? Trust is a design requirement, not a feature.

The inception

2020

Going global

Expansion across Asia, Australia and Europe brings Metquay to labs on three continents.

2023

The refined product brand launches — works, worksheets, certificates and traceability in one connected system.
