## Got questions?We’ve got answers.

01

### Is metquay.ai compliant with ISO 17025 and FDA 21 CFR Part 11?

Yes. Every measurement, procedure version, signature and approval is captured in an immutable, version-pinned audit trail. Digital signatures require re-authentication for 21 CFR Part 11, and certificates output as accredited PDF/A.

02

### How long does migration from our current system take?

Most labs go live in 10–20 weeks with a structured, parallel-run migration, never a big-bang cutover. We support Qualer, GagePack, IndySoft and MET/TEAM with per-platform playbooks and full compliance continuity.

03

### Can technicians really work fully offline?

Yes. metquay.ai is an offline-first PWA. Worksheets, assets and procedures are stored locally; all changes queue in an outbox and sync automatically with conflict resolution when connectivity returns.

04

### Which ERP and accounting systems do you integrate with?

Odoo, Oracle NetSuite, QuickBooks (Online & Desktop), Microsoft Dynamics 365, Business Central and Xero, plus Fluke MET/CAL via MET/Connect and metrology.net for orders. REST APIs and webhooks cover anything custom.

05

### How does the AI interval optimization stay compliant?

It rolls out in staged phases — observation, review, trial, then full activation. Every AI-driven interval change requires a recommendation report, risk assessment, QA Manager approval and an immutable audit entry.
